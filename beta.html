What's Inside VigilChild Beta?

1. Call Recording Beta Feature

Automatically records both incoming and outgoing phone calls placed on the child's device. Audio recordings are encrypted and synchronized exclusively to your Parent controller phone via secure P2P without permanent server retention.

2. Live Audio Listening While Calling Live Sync

Allows parents to establish a low-latency live audio streaming channel during an active phone call to listen to real-time ambient surroundings and protect children from predatory contacts, bullying, or dangerous situations.

How to Set Up the Beta Environment

Ensure both your Parent controller and Child companion app are in sync.

1

Join VigilParent Beta on Google Play

On your parent phone, visit the VigilParent listing on Google Play Store. Scroll down to "Join the beta program" and tap Join. Once updated, you will have the Beta dashboard ready.

Step Required First
2

Download & Install VigilChild 3.0 Beta APK

On the child’s device, check the consent box above, download the v3.0 Beta APK (54.8 MB), and enable "Allow from this source" if prompted by Android.

3

Grant Audio & Call Permissions

Open VigilChild Beta on the child device. Grant requested Microphone and Call Log permissions so the app can detect call events and capture audio for parental safety.

4

Pair Devices via QR Code

Open your updated Parent app, generate a new connection QR code, and scan it with the Child Beta app to complete the encrypted P2P channel handshake.
