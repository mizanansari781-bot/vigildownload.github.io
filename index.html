How to Install on Your Child's Device

1

Download the App

Tap the download button above and save the APK file to this device.

2

Disable Play Protect (Temporary)

Open the Google Play Store > Tap the Profile Icon > Play Protect > Settings (Gear). Turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect". This is required for custom system-monitoring apps.

3

Allow Unknown Sources

Open the downloaded APK file. If prompted, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source" so your browser can install the app.

4

Install and Pair

Complete the installation, open VigilChild, grant the requested Accessibility permissions, and scan the pairing QR code from your Parent App.
