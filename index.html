How to Install on Your Child's Device

Follow these 4 simple steps to complete setup and pair with your parent phone.

1

Download the APK File

Check the consent box above, tap the Download APK button, and save the file to this Android device.

2

Disable Google Play Protect (Temporary)

Open Google Play Store > tap your Profile Icon > Play Protect > Settings (Gear Icon). Temporarily turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect". This prevents false alerts during installation of specialized parental supervision tools.

💡 You can turn it back on after pairing is complete
3

Allow Unknown Source Installation

Open your file manager or browser downloads and tap the VigilChild.2.7.apk file. If prompted with a security dialog, tap Settings and switch on "Allow from this source".

4

Install, Grant Permissions & Pair

Complete the package installation and open VigilChild. Follow the on-screen prompts to grant Accessibility and Location permissions, then scan the pairing QR code displayed in your VigilParent controller app.

P2P Encrypted Sync

Activity logs transfer directly between family devices over encrypted P2P. No activity logs are stored on cloud servers.

Zero Server Storage

Screen time, browser history, and application logs are never uploaded to company cloud databases.

Offline-First Caching

When internet is unavailable, logs remain encrypted locally on the child device and sync automatically when reconnected.
